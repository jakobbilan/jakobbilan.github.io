The Rental Adaptation Gap: Split Incentives in Private Adaptation to Heat

Type
Working paper, in preparation
Coauthors
Marc Ringel (Sciences Po)
Description
Asks whether the split incentive between landlords and tenants holds back private adaptation to heat, measured as household investment in cooling equipment, by comparing renters with owners across the EU-27.
Data
EIB Climate Survey 2024 (EU-27); external validation with US federal surveys (RECS 2020, AHS 2015–2023)

Determinants of Altruistic Willingness-to-Pay (WTP) for an Environmental Tax: Evidence from Germany, Sweden and Italy

Type
Group analytical report, EIB Climate Hackathon 2025
Coauthors
Robert Birkmayer, Ewa Jarosz, Etienne Oriot, Clara Simões Coelho (all Sciences Po)
Description
Examines which factors predict willingness to pay an extra income tax to finance climate policies that benefit people on lower incomes than themselves. Country-by-country ordinal logit models (partial proportional odds where the parallel-slopes assumption fails) identify climate concern and trust in government as the strongest positive predictors; respondents aged 50 and over, and those further to the political right, are less willing to pay.
Data
EIB Climate Survey 2023 (Germany, Sweden, Italy); Eurostat income quintile share ratio (S80/S20) by NUTS 2 region
