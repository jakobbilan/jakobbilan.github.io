Determinants of Altruistic Willingness-to-Pay (WTP) for an Environmental Tax: Evidence from Germany, Sweden and Italy

Type
Group analytical report, EIB Climate Hackathon 2025
Coauthors
Robert Birkmayer, Ewa Jarosz, Etienne Oriot, Clara Simões Coelho (all Sciences Po)
Description
Examines what predicts willingness to pay an extra income tax for climate policies that benefit people on lower incomes, using country-by-country ordinal logit models. Climate concern and trust in government are the strongest positive predictors; respondents aged 50 and over and those further to the political right are less willing to pay.
Data
EIB Climate Survey 2023 (Germany, Sweden, Italy); Eurostat income quintile share ratio (S80/S20) by NUTS 2 region
