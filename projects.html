Type
Team policy project and policy brief
Programme

Addressed to
France’s energy and climate directorate (DGEC)
Period
Spring 2026
Description
Controversy mapping and policy recommendations on how Cigéo’s costs are estimated and financed, including the discount rate nuclear operators use to provision for them.
