Paddy-Residue Burning in Punjab

Type
Team policy project, Strategic Policymaking (PP425), LSE MPA
Role
Member of a seven-person team; co-drafted the Clean Field Contract
Convenor
Luis Garicano (LSE)
Period
Autumn 2026
Description
How Punjab can stop farmers burning paddy straw after the harvest, which left burn scars on about 60% of the state’s paddy area in 2024 and feeds north India’s post-monsoon air pollution. The team compares the Clean Field Contract, a payment for fields left unburnt and verified by satellite, with creating a market for the straw.
