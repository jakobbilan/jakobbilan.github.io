Jakob Kilian Bilan

I study economic policy in a double master’s degree at the London School of Economics (MPA) and Sciences Po (MPP), and work as a research assistant at the LSE Growth Lab and at Sciences Po’s European Chair for Sustainable Development and Climate Transition.

My research interests are distributional climate policy, quasi-experimental policy evaluation and EU sustainable finance. Before my master’s, I worked on development finance at KfW in Phnom Penh, on financial services at the Permanent Representation of Germany to the EU in Brussels, and on modernising public administration at PD in Berlin.

Latest project: England’s council tax is stuck in 1991, an interactive comparison of what every small area in England pays with what its homes are worth today.
