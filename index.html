I am a master’s student in the LSE-Sciences Po double degree in Public Administration (MPA, Economic Policy) and Public Policy (MPP, Economics).

I currently work as a research assistant at the LSE Growth Lab and at Sciences Po’s European Chair for Sustainable Development and Climate Transition. My research spans economic and fiscal policy, climate change, development, trade and more.

My latest data science project can be found here: England’s council tax is stuck in 1991, an interactive comparison of what every small area in England pays with what its homes are worth today.
